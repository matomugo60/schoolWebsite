About Prestige University

Founded in 1950, Prestige University has been at the forefront of academic excellence, fostering innovation and leadership for over seven decades.

Our 200-acre campus provides state-of-the-art facilities and a nurturing environment for over 5,000 students from 60 countries worldwide.

We are proud to be consistently ranked among the top 1% of universities globally.
